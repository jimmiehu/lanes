# Meeting template

Copied into a daily note when a meeting happens, then filled in. [[2026-10-07|Wednesday's]] plot meeting was written from it. The folder name starts with `_`, so the rail's Folders view sorts it last, with the rest of the scaffolding.

## Meeting — the topic

Date: the day, and where or how
With: who was there, as links to their notes

### Agenda

- What it was called for

### Notes

What was said, in sentences.

### Decisions

1. What was decided, one per line.

### Actions

- Who does what, by when. Make each one a task with ⌘↵ once it is copied out, so ⌘K lists it.
