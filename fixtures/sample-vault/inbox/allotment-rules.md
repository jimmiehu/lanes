# Allotment rules, as clipped

Clipped from the association's website by a browser extension, which drops what it clips into this folder. This sample marks `inbox/` read-only: Lanes shows these notes and never writes to them — no edits, no renames, no moves, no trash. A note made with ⌘N never lands here either.

Anything worth keeping goes into a note of your own, in your words, and a link back here says where it came from.

## The rules, as clipped

1. Plots are to be cultivated. At least three quarters of each plot must be in use for growing.
2. No permanent structures without written permission, other than one shed and one cold frame.
3. Hoses may be used for filling water butts only.
4. Bonfires are not permitted on any plot at any time.
5. Paths between plots are to be kept clear and cut.
6. Dogs on leads. No other animals without permission.
7. Rent is due on the first of October.

Monday 5 October, from the association's page of rules.
