# Garden charter — what the plot is for

Plot 14 at the allotments by the river, shared with [[ada-lovelace|Ada]] since spring. This note is the one we come back to when we disagree about what to grow.

## What it is for

1. Vegetables we would otherwise buy every week: leeks, beans, salad, garlic.
2. One thing each year we have never grown. This year it is celeriac.
3. Somewhere to be on a Saturday morning that is not a screen.

## What it is not for

- Show produce. Nothing is grown for size.
- Anything that needs watering every day in August. We are away for two weeks of it.
- Lawn.

## How we decide

Whoever does the work on a bed decides what goes in it, and writes it in [[beds]]. Disagreements wait until the next Saturday, which usually settles them.

The association's rules are in the inbox — [Allotment rules](inbox/allotment-rules.md) — clipped from their site and not ours to edit.
