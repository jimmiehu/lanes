# Compost — the three bays

Three bays made of pallets, at the back of the plot by the hedge. Material goes in at the left and moves right as it rots.

| Bay | Holds | Turned |
|---|---|---|
| Left | This season's waste, going in | Never — it is still being filled |
| Middle | Last season's, half done | Once in spring, once in autumn |
| Right | Ready, or nearly | Not again; it is used from here |

## What goes in

- Spent crops, cut up. Whole stems sit there for years.
- Grass from the paths, in thin layers between drier stuff.
- Cardboard, torn, without tape.
- Kitchen peelings from [[grace-hopper|Grace]], a bucket a week.

## What does not

- Blighted tomato plants. They go in the council bin.
- Perennial weeds with roots. They go in a bucket of water first, for a month.
- Anything cooked.

## The rule of thumb

Equal amounts green and brown, by volume, and wet as a wrung-out sponge. When it smells, add brown. When nothing happens, add green.

The right-hand bay feeds the [[beds]] in November.
