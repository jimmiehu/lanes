# Bread — the weekly loaf

## The dough

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Strong white flour | 450 g |
| Wholemeal | 50 g |
| Water | 360 g |
| Starter | 100 g |
| Salt | 10 g |

## The week

1. Thursday night: feed the starter.
2. Friday, eight in the morning: mix flour and water, leave for an hour.
3. Add starter and salt. Four folds, half an hour apart.
4. Shape before bed and leave in the fridge overnight.
5. Saturday: bake from cold, lid on for twenty minutes, off for twenty-five.

## Notes

- A cold kitchen in winter adds an hour to step 3. Do not add yeast to make up for it.
- The loaf for the [[saturday-menu|Saturday table]] is the second one; the first is ours.
- [[grace-hopper|Grace]] keeps a back-up of the starter in Oslo, which has been needed once.
