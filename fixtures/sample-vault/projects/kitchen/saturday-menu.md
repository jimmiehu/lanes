## What the marks are

Each comment above is two pieces of markup:

- `{==…==}` is the anchor, the words a comment is about.
- `{>>…<<}` is the comment. One with no anchor in front of it is a comment at a point.

READ washes an anchor's words and puts the comment in the margin beside its line; in a narrow pane each comment is a marker you click. To write one, select some words and choose `comment` beside `quote`, or press ⌥⌘M. A comment pressed opens with a ✓, which resolves it: the comment goes and its anchor's words stay.

⌘K finds a comment by its words, under COMMENTS. And the details panel's **Comments** row offers *go through*: a new thread in which Claude reads the note and answers each one.

Comments do not print. The PDF of this note has the anchors' words and none of the comments.
