# Stock, and what goes in it

A pot on the back of the stove on Sunday, made from what the week left over. Frozen in pint tubs, labelled with the date and nothing else.

## Vegetable

Onion skins, leek tops, celery ends, parsley stalks, a carrot past its best. Not brassicas: cabbage and kale make it bitter. Cover with cold water, simmer an hour, strain, reduce by a third.

## Chicken

The carcass from the Saturday roast, an onion cut in half, a bay leaf, peppercorns. Simmer for three hours with the lid half on. Skim at the start and leave it alone after.

## What it is for

- The soup on the [[saturday-menu|Saturday menu]].
- Risotto on a weeknight.
- Lentils cooked in it rather than in water, which is most of the reason to make it.

> A good stock is mostly patience and a pot that is too big.
> — written on the inside of the cupboard door, in pencil

The vegetable scraps that do not go in the pot go to the [[compost]].
